The short version

I started doing this because I kept watching capable UAE businesses buy AI the wrong way: seats for ChatGPT bought before anyone mapped the workflow, English-only tools pointed at an Arabic-first customer base, and "transformation" measured in screenshots instead of dirhams.

So I built the opposite practice. Every engagement starts with the workflow, prices the outcome in AED, integrates Arabic properly, and reports savings you can audit. It's a solo practice on purpose — you work with the person who does the work.

And in 2026 I started building, not just advising: Cart402, an agentic-commerce product on Base where AI agents shop and settle in USDC onchain. The practice funds the product; the product sharpens the practice.
