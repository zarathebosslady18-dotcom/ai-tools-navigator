# 60-second Calendly intro video — script

> The first thing a qualified suitor hears is the story that makes them lean in.
> Format: one take, face-to-camera, standing or seated, good light, phone or laptop cam. No slides. ~150 words = 60 seconds.

---

**[0–8s — hook]**
"Most UAE businesses don't have an AI problem. They have a measurement problem — they bought the tools first and the answers second."

**[8–20s — proof]**
"I'm [name] — I run an AI transformation practice in Dubai. Five hundred businesses through my door, and the number that matters is one: the average one saves over five thousand dirhams a month. Not 'efficiency' — dirhams, measured for thirty days, auditable."

**[20–34s — the edge]**
"Here's what makes this different in this market: I don't sell tools. I price the outcome in AED before any software exists, I integrate Arabic properly — dialect in, MSA out — and if it doesn't clear the bar in thirty days, we kill it. That discipline is the product."

**[34–48s — the lean-in]**
"And I'm not just advising anymore. I'm building — agentic commerce on Base, where AI agents shop within budgets and settle onchain in USDC. The practice funds the product, and the product is where I'm raising."

**[48–60s — the ask]**
"So if you're building in the Gulf, raising in the agent economy, or you have a workflow that costs you real dirhams — book the thirty minutes. Bring the workflow that hurts. We'll find out if AI pays for itself there — in numbers, not buzzwords. I'm [name]. The link's right there."

---

## Delivery notes
- Speak ~15% slower than feels natural; pause on "dirhams, measured for thirty days, auditable."
- The two numbers (500 businesses / AED 5,000) must be said deadpan — they're the credibility, not the hype.
- "We kill it" is the line that makes founders lean in — let it land.
- If the venture is Cart402, the 48s ask is your closer. If the venture you're leading is different, swap lines 34–48 for it — the structure (proof → edge → lean-in → ask) stays identical.
- Record 3 takes, send the second one.
